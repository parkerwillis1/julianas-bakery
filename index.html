Our story

A studio built on patience

J Bake Co. started in a small kitchen with one oven, a stack of borrowed tins, and a stubborn belief that a cake should taste as considered as it looks. Juliana still bakes every order herself.

We take a handful of cakes each week — never more — so that each one gets the attention it was ordered for.
