A note from the baker

Baking, and then designing

I have been baking since I was tall enough to reach the counter, which by my mother's account was several years before I was tall enough to be trusted near the oven. The love came first. The craft took considerably longer.

J Bake Co. grew out of that, a place where a cake is designed the way a room is designed. I start with what the day is actually about, then work backwards: the palette, the proportions, the one detail that makes people lean in. Restraint does most of the work. A cake does not need to announce itself to be remembered.

A cake should look like it belongs to the people eating it.

Everything is made to order in small numbers, by hand, in one kitchen. I source seasonally because it tastes better and because a cherry in June simply looks like a cherry in June. Butter, flour, sugar, time. Nothing clever, just done carefully.

If you are planning something, I would love to hear about it. The best cakes I have made started with a long conversation and a very rough sketch on the back of an envelope.
